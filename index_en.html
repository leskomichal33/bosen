How I will sell your property

A listing on a portal is not enough. The buyer has to be found.

Together we choose the options that suit your property, its location and the target buyer.

01

Property presentation

  • Professional photos and videoA photographer with a wide-angle lens and the right lighting.
  • Drone footageFor houses, plots and cottages it shows the surroundings, access roads and the neighbourhood.
  • 3D virtual tourBuyers walk through the property online, so the people who come to viewings already know it.
  • Floor plan and 2D/3D visualisationBuyers can picture the layout.
  • Home stagingBasic (tidying, depersonalising, light furnishing) or full staging with rented furniture.
  • Renovation visualisationFor a property in need of work it shows its potential, including AI visualisation.
02

Paid online advertising

  • Facebook and InstagramTargeting by location, age and interests, including audiences similar to our clients.
  • RetargetingThe ad is shown again to people who have already viewed your listing.
  • Google Ads and YouTubeReach people who are actively searching, e.g. “apartment for sale Malacky”. Also short video tours before YouTube videos.
  • SklikAds in the Seznam search engine as a complement to Google.
  • Featured listings on portalsHighlighting and premium placement on Nehnutelnosti.sk, Topreality.sk and Reality.sk.
03

Offline and local activities

  • Open houseSeveral interested buyers view the property at the same time.
  • Leaflets in the neighbourhoodWe reach households in the immediate vicinity.
  • “For sale” sign with a QR codeRight on the property, linking to the full listing.
  • Off-market sale to our clients firstBefore it goes public, I send the offer to my own database of buyers – by e-mail, SMS or WhatsApp.
04

Video, content and referrals

  • Reels and TikTok videosShort tours telling the story of the property or location (30–60 s).
  • “What you get for this price” videoA comparison of properties or locations that grabs attention and gets shared.
  • Articles about the locationNurseries, transport and housing prices – so your listing can also be found via search engines.
  • Client referralsVideo testimonials and Google reviews that build trust.
05

Partnerships and wider reach

  • Other agentsI also share the offer with colleagues who have their own buyers.
  • Mortgage advisors and developersThey refer clients who already have an approved mortgage.
  • Investors and companiesFor larger properties: investors, local employers or relocation agencies.
  • Listing in EnglishFor foreigners and expats looking for a home in Slovakia.
